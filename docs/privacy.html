MySchool Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{DATE}}

MySchool ("the app") is a school management app run by {{OWNER_NAME}} ("we"). Schools use it to share results, attendance, fees records, notices, meetings and private messages with their students, parents and staff. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Who is responsible for the data

Each school decides what information about its students, parents and staff is put into the app, and is responsible for it. We provide the app and store the data on the school's behalf. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that apply.

What data is handled

  • Account details: name, email (staff and parents) or admission number and PIN (students), and your role in your school.
  • School records entered by the school: class, test and exam scores, grades, teacher remarks, attendance, fees set and payments the school recorded, and optional personal details such as date of birth, gender and home address.
  • Communication: notices, parent-teacher meeting details and replies, notifications, and private messages between parents and teachers.
  • School information: school name, logo, motto and contact details.
  • Activity records: an audit log of important actions (for example approving results), kept for security and accountability.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos (except a school logo chosen by a school administrator), advertising identifiers, or card and bank details. It contains no advertising and no analytics trackers.

Why we use the data

Only to run the app for your school: to sign you in, show you the right information, notify you about notices, meetings and messages, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data.

Who can see it

  • Each school's data is kept separate from all other schools by access rules in the database.
  • Students and parents see only their own (or their child's) information. Teachers see only the classes they teach. School administrators see their own school's information.
  • Private messages between a parent and a teacher cannot be read by the school unless a participant reports a message; then the school's administrators can read that conversation to deal with the report.
  • Our hosting provider, Supabase, stores and processes the data for us (database, sign-in and file storage). We do not share data with other third parties.

Children

Students may be children. Their accounts are created by their school, which is responsible for having the right to do so. Parents can ask the school about what is stored about their child.

How long we keep data

For as long as the school uses the app, or as the school instructs us. When a school leaves the service, its data is deleted or returned on request.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Access is limited by role and by school. Student PINs are protected against repeated guessing. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password or PIN private and change it if you think someone knows it.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to how it is used. Ask your school administrator first, or contact us and we will pass your request to your school and help. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and records, ask your school administrator, or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your name, school and admission number or email. We normally respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell schools through the app.
